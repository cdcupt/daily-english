1. Overview

一、概述

Dailingo ("the App") is an AI scenario-based English trainer. You practice English through real-world scenarios, receive focused AI corrections, and build a personal expression bank alongside a CEFR ability profile. We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and how you stay in control. It applies to both the iOS app and the web client at english.daichenlab.com.

Dailingo（以下简称"本应用"）是一款基于 AI 的情景化英语训练应用。您可以通过真实场景练习英语、获得有针对性的 AI 纠错，并积累个人表达库与 CEFR 能力画像。我们非常重视您的隐私。本隐私政策说明我们收集哪些信息、如何使用，以及您如何掌控这些数据。本政策同时适用于 iOS 应用与网页客户端 english.daichenlab.com。

2. Information We Collect

二、我们收集的信息

Account Information

Dailingo is login-first. You sign in with Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. We receive your email address and a user/account identifier from these providers. This is linked to your account and used to provide the service (App Functionality) — to save your progress, expression bank, and ability profile across devices.

Practice Content

The practice answers you submit (translations, role-play replies, topic descriptions, and related speech transcripts) are sent to our server and processed by an AI provider to generate corrections and scores. This User Content is linked to your account and used for App Functionality.

Microphone & Speech

For speaking practice, the App uses your microphone together with on-device speech recognition to convert your speech to text. Audio is processed on your device; the resulting transcript may be sent to the server for grading like any other practice answer.

账户信息

Dailingo 采用登录优先模式。您可以通过 使用 Apple 登录 或 使用 Google 登录 进入应用。我们会从这些服务商获取您的电子邮箱地址和一个用户/账户标识符。该信息与您的账户关联，仅用于提供服务（应用功能）——在多设备间保存您的学习进度、表达库与能力画像。

练习内容

您提交的练习答案（翻译、角色扮演对话、话题描述及相关的语音转写文本）会发送至我们的服务器，并由 AI 服务商处理以生成纠错与评分。这些用户内容与您的账户关联，用于实现应用功能。

麦克风与语音

在口语练习中，本应用会使用您的麦克风并配合设备端语音识别将语音转换为文字。音频在您的设备上处理；转写得到的文本可能会像其他练习答案一样发送至服务器进行评分。

3. How Your Data Is Stored

三、数据如何存储

Your account, progress, expression bank, and practice history are stored on our server (over HTTPS) and tied to your account so they stay in sync across the iOS app and the web client. We use standard HTTPS encryption in transit (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false; no proprietary or non-exempt encryption).

您的账户、学习进度、表达库与练习记录存储在我们的服务器上（通过 HTTPS 传输），并与您的账户关联，以便在 iOS 应用与网页客户端之间保持同步。数据在传输过程中采用标准 HTTPS 加密（ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false；不使用专有或非豁免加密技术）。

4. Third-Party Services

四、第三方服务

We rely on a small number of trusted third parties strictly to make the App work:

我们仅为实现应用功能而使用少数受信任的第三方服务：

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple (authentication).
  • Google — Sign in with Google (authentication).
  • OpenAI — our server's AI provider, which processes your submitted practice text to generate corrections and scores.
  • Apple — 使用 Apple 登录（身份验证）。
  • Google — 使用 Google 登录（身份验证）。
  • OpenAI — 我们服务器使用的 AI 服务商，用于处理您提交的练习文本以生成纠错与评分。

We do not use third-party advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

我们不使用第三方广告，不会在其他应用或网站上追踪您，也不会出售您的数据。

5. Device Permissions

五、设备权限

  • Microphone — for recording speech during speaking practice (only when you actively record). You can deny this and still use text-based practice.
  • Speech Recognition — for converting your speech to text on-device (Apple's recognition).
  • Network Access — for signing in and sending practice answers to our server for AI grading.
  • 麦克风 — 用于口语练习中的语音录制（仅在您主动录音时使用）。您可以拒绝此权限，仍可正常使用文字练习。
  • 语音识别 — 用于在设备端将您的语音转换为文字（使用 Apple 语音识别）。
  • 网络访问 — 用于登录以及将练习答案发送至服务器进行 AI 评分。

6. Deleting Your Account & Data

六、删除您的账户与数据

You stay in control. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, email us at cdcupt@gmail.com from your account email. You may also sign out at any time, and you can deny microphone/speech access while still using text practice. See our Your Privacy Choices page for details.

您始终掌控自己的数据。如需删除您的账户及所有关联数据，请使用您的账户邮箱发送邮件至 cdcupt@gmail.com。您也可以随时退出登录；并且可以在拒绝麦克风/语音权限的同时继续使用文字练习。详情请参阅 您的隐私选择 页面。

本应用不专门面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。
