You stay in control of your data in Dailingo. This page summarizes the choices available to you. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

在 Dailingo 中，您始终掌控自己的数据。本页面汇总了您可使用的各项选择。完整说明请参阅 隐私政策。

What You Can Control

您可以控制的内容

Delete your account & data

Email us at cdcupt@gmail.com from your account email and we will delete your account and all associated data — your progress, Expression Bank, and practice history.

删除您的账户与数据

请使用您的账户邮箱发送邮件至 cdcupt@gmail.com，我们将删除您的账户及所有关联数据——包括学习进度、表达库和练习记录。

Deny microphone & speech — keep practicing

Speaking practice uses your microphone with on-device speech recognition. You can deny these permissions and still use text-based practice (translation, role-play, topic description). The choice is yours, and you can change it anytime in iOS Settings.

拒绝麦克风与语音——仍可继续练习

口语练习会使用麦克风并配合设备端语音识别。您可以拒绝这些权限，仍然使用文字练习（翻译、角色扮演、话题描述）。选择权在您，且可随时在 iOS 设置中更改。

Sign out

You can sign out at any time from within the app. Signing in again (with Apple or Google) restores your synced data.

退出登录

您可以随时在应用内退出登录。再次登录（使用 Apple 或 Google）即可恢复已同步的数据。

No tracking. No ads. No data sale.

不追踪。不投广告。不出售数据。

We do not use third-party advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data. We collect only what's needed to run the app: your account email and a user identifier (via Sign in with Apple/Google), and the practice answers you submit for AI grading.

我们不使用第三方广告，不会在其他应用或网站上追踪您，也绝不出售您的数据。我们只收集运行应用所必需的信息：您的账户邮箱与用户标识符（通过使用 Apple/Google 登录），以及您提交评分的练习答案。
