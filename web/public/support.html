Getting Started

快速上手

  1. Sign in with Apple or Google (Dailingo is login-first, so your progress syncs across devices).
  2. Pick a scenario or topic — choose from category chips (ordering food, travel, small talk, workplace, job interviews…) or ask the AI to generate a custom topic.
  3. Practice in one of three modes: Translation practice, Role-play dialogue, or Topic description. Use text, or tap the mic to practice speaking.
  4. Get focused corrections — the AI surfaces only the 1–3 issues that matter most for your level, plus a CEFR-aligned score.
  5. Review — useful words, patterns, and recurring mistakes are auto-saved to your Expression Bank, then resurfaced through spaced review.
  1. 登录：使用 Apple 或 Google 登录（Dailingo 采用登录优先模式，您的进度会在多设备间同步）。
  2. 选择场景或话题：从分类标签中挑选（点餐、旅行、闲聊、职场、求职面试……），或让 AI 为您生成一个自定义话题。
  3. 开始练习：在三种模式中任选其一——翻译练习、角色扮演对话或话题描述。可使用文字输入，也可点击麦克风进行口语练习。
  4. 获得有针对性的纠错：AI 只会指出最关键的 1–3 个问题（与您的水平匹配），并给出符合 CEFR 的评分。
  5. 复习：有用的词汇、句型和反复出现的错误会自动保存到您的表达库，并通过间隔复习再次出现。

FAQ

常见问题

How do I sign in?

如何登录？

Open the app and tap "Sign in with Apple" or "Sign in with Google". An account keeps your progress, Expression Bank, and ability profile in sync across the iOS app and the web client at english.daichenlab.com.

打开应用后，点击「使用 Apple 登录」或「使用 Google 登录」。登录后，您的进度、表达库与能力画像会在 iOS 应用与网页客户端 english.daichenlab.com 之间保持同步。

Do I need an API key?

需要 API 密钥吗？

No. v2.0 is server-backed — the AI is built in and runs on our server. You no longer need to bring your own API key like the previous version.

不需要。v2.0 采用服务器后端，AI 已内置并运行在我们的服务器上。您无需再像旧版本那样自带 API 密钥。

How is my level scored?

我的水平如何评分？

After each practice the AI gives an explainable score and builds a CEFR ability profile (A1–C2) with weak-point detail. Please note this is an AI estimate to guide your learning — it is not an official CEFR certification.

每次练习后，AI 会给出可解释的评分，并构建包含薄弱点细节的 CEFR 能力画像（A1–C2）。请注意，这只是用于辅助学习的 AI 估算，并非官方 CEFR 认证。

Can I practice speaking?

可以练习口语吗？

Yes. Dailingo uses your microphone with on-device speech recognition so you can practice pronunciation and fluency out loud. Prefer to type? You can deny mic access and still use text practice.

可以。Dailingo 会使用您的麦克风并配合设备端语音识别，让您可以朗读练习发音与流利度。如果更喜欢打字，您可以拒绝麦克风权限，仍然使用文字练习。

How do I delete my account or data?

如何删除我的账户或数据？

Email us at cdcupt@gmail.com from your account email and we will delete your account and all associated data. See Your Privacy Choices for more.

请使用您的账户邮箱发送邮件至 cdcupt@gmail.com，我们将删除您的账户及所有关联数据。更多信息请参阅 您的隐私选择。

Is my data private?

我的数据私密吗？

Yes. We only collect what's needed to run the app (your account email and the practice you submit for grading). No third-party ads, no tracking across apps, and we never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy.

是的。我们只收集运行应用所必需的信息（您的账户邮箱以及您提交评分的练习内容）。没有第三方广告、不跨应用追踪，也绝不出售您的数据。详见 隐私政策。
