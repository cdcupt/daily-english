Meet Cleo, the AI coach.

One owned, flat-geometric cat in the warm editorial palette — teal body, coral nose, a violet AI spark. Not an emoji. Two expressions, coach and celebrate, defined once as reusable SVG symbols and rendered below at 256 / 96 / 48 / 32px on light and dark to prove she still reads as a cat at in-app avatar sizes (48 / 32).
